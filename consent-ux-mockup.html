3 · Stopping it (honest about what revocation does)
Stop sharing with Willamette Vital Health?
They'll receive no further updates, starting now.

Information they've already seen or saved can't be pulled back — the same as any record you've shared. Stopping prevents anything new from being shared.

↑ No false promises. Revocation stops the future and best-effort deletes the latest bundle; it can't retract a cached snapshot, and the screen says so. Every grant, refresh, and revocation lands in the tamper-evident audit log.
